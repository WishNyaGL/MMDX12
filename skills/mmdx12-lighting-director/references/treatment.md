# Treatment template

Fill this in before you build a rig. Write in feeling words; the technical choices come after.

## The form

```
Song / artist:
What it is about (3-5 lines):
Emotional arc, and where it peaks:

Logline of the lighting idea (1-2 sentences):

Key / fill / back philosophy:
   Where is the key, what does the fill do, what does the back reveal?
   What can the camera see, and what stays in darkness?

Colour script:
   section        palette            why
   ------------   ----------------   --------------------------------
   intro
   verse
   pre-chorus
   chorus
   break
   bridge
   final chorus
   outro

Contrast level (how much darkness this film can afford):

The moments light changes the most (and what happens):

Singer policy (follow spots, solos, unison, breaths):

Restraint — what I will NOT do in this film:
   -
   -
```

## Example A — intimate solo ballad

Song: a slow piano ballad, one singer, 3:40, no dancers.

What it is about: someone singing to a person who has left the room. The arc is denial to admission, peaking on the
last chorus when the voice stops being careful.

Logline: a single lamp in a black room, and a person who cannot step out of it.

Key / fill / back: one warm key, low and close on the camera side, so the face is modelled and one cheek is dark.
No fill — the darkness is the scene partner. A faint cool rim separates her shoulder from the black. The rest of the
stage does not exist.

Colour script:

| Section | Palette | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| intro | amber, almost no colour | a room at night, one lamp |
| verse | amber, slightly cooler as it goes | the light loses certainty with her |
| pre-chorus | amber + a cold blue rim | the first doubt enters at the edge |
| chorus | amber core, blue rim grows | warmth and distance in the same frame |
| final chorus | the rim overtakes the key | the room has become the absence |
| outro | key slowly down to a single edge | she is almost gone |

Contrast: very high. The background is black; nothing else is lit. Exposure is carried by the face.

Moments: at 2:31 (last "stay") the key swells 15% over 40 frames and does not pulse. At 3:02 the blue rim brightens
as the key dims — the only colour change in the final minute.

Singer policy: one follow spot on the head, but it IS the key; no other spot. During breaths it falls by a third.
There is no unison, so no group wash.

Restraint: no strobe, no beams, no colour cycling, no second key, nothing that moves faster than the voice.

## Example B — high-energy group idol chorus

Song: 5-member dance track, 3:10, four-on-the-floor, drop at 0:58.

What it is about: five people deciding tonight is the best night of their lives. The arc is anticipation,
explosion, joy finding new gears.

Logline: the stage is a nightclub and the group is the only light source that matters.

Key / fill / back: a bright front wash keeps every face readable; the drama comes from behind. Beams from the back
and above throw haze and colour into the camera. The key is cool white so the skin stays true while the beams are
saturated.

Colour script:

| Section | Palette | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| intro | deep blue, one white top light | the room before the doors open |
| verse | blue + a little magenta | anticipation, restrained |
| pre-chorus | blue -> cyan, beams rising | the lift is visible before it is heard |
| chorus | cyan + magenta, white key | the drop is a colour event |
| break | magenta + white strobe (1 bar) | peak, then a breath |
| final chorus | cyan + magenta + warm white | the same chorus, bigger |
| outro | magenta fading to a single white | the night ends on the group, not the dark |

Contrast: medium. The background is never black in the chorus — the beams and haze fill it — but the verse keeps the
edges dark.

Moments: at 0:58 the back beams snap to full and the key steps up one stop. At 2:37 the beams converge into a V over
the group and the wash goes warm.

Singer policy: one follow spot per member, all keyed from `studio_singing`. A solo line gets its spot up 6 frames
before and the other four dim 20%; unison returns them to the group wash. Held notes swell.

Restraint: no strobe in the verses, no colour change every bar, no rainbow, no light that ignores who is singing.

## What makes a treatment usable

- It names a feeling per section, so a pattern choice has something to serve.
- It has a contrast level, so the intensity budget has a target.
- It has restraint. A film with no "will not" is a pile of options.
